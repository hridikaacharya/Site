Three Days in Karkineta

On July 27 we travelled from Kathmandu to Karkineta, Parbat, and spent three days with the community.

We spent the visit on three topics: women's empowerment, financial literacy and reproductive health awareness.

We came back with new connections, honest conversations and a stronger commitment to giving back.

"Project Parbat was an opportunity to share knowledge, learn from the community, and take a small step towards positive change."
