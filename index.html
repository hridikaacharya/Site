About Pragya Nepal

What We Do

Pragya Nepal is run by students. We plan and lead outreach projects in communities that get very little health information: school workshops, health camps and awareness drives. Where we can, we also hand out health supplies.

What Isn't Talked About

In many places in Nepal, periods, reproductive health and money are not things girls can talk about openly. Many grow up without accurate information, and some miss school or leave it because of that.

Why Students

We are students too, so we can talk to girls our own age in a way adults often can't. We start with basic health education, then move on to money skills, studying and technical skills. We want to show that young Nepalis can run this kind of outreach themselves.
